We respond to DC’s three main points: (1) whether our *in-situ* HERFD-XAS experiments achieved the dissolution and complexation equilibria relevant to the species we identified; (2) whether the new Nb2O5 solubility data of [Liu et al. (2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S001670372600356X?via%3Dihub#b0035) are consistent with our proposed high-order Nb-F-O(H) complexes; and (3) whether the geological conclusions of [Hu et al. (2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S001670372600356X?via%3Dihub#b0025) are undermined by the high-F conditions of some of our XAS runs. We conclude with brief comments for future work.
